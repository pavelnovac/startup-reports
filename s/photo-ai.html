Should you build this

Photo AI generates pictures of you from selfies. Through 2025 the posts climbed toward $150k MRR. The October 2026 bio says $86k. Interior AI, same founder, fell from over $50k to $21k. HeadshotPro's $300k month is a late-2024 article the founder later walked back. Boothie, a cousin that re-renders a selfie as a digicam photo, is a real smaller Stripe balance — and it is still the same idea. The year for this already happened.
