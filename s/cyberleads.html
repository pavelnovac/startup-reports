Should you build this

CyberLeads sent B2B lead lists every month. The founder posted the whole life of it: a climb to $40k MRR in 2024, a note that 2024 cut revenue in half, and $1,083 in September 2026. That is the rare chart that argues against its own idea. Don't build a done-for-you list business and expect the 2024 peak to be the market.
