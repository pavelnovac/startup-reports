Should you build this

Fly Pieter is a browser flight game, vibe-coded, monetized with in-game ads. The entire revenue trail is one week in March 2025: $57k, $67k, then $87k MRR and '$0 to $1M ARR in 17 days'. The report notes the churn, and the bio moved on to a different URL. A launch week can collect money. It is not a company to clone.

Cited from the captured posts, shown below: the earliest captured post (Mar 5, 2025) is the source for “$57K MRR”; the latest milestone (Mar 12, 2025) is the source for “$0 to $1M ARR in 17 days ($87K MRR)”.
