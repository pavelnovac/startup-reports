Should you build this

Therapists need a calendar, a payment, and a place the client comes back to. Calendesk sells those as one product, $21.6k MRR, verified, with a ten-point history. That is a profession, not a platform. Pick a licensed job in one country — and not therapists, if that slot is already staffed — and own the appointment plus the invoice. Another horizontal Calendly is not this idea.
