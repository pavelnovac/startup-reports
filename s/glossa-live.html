Should you build this

Glossa translates a church service, live, into a lot of languages, and charges by the hour from $5. Verified MRR is about $13.6k, founded in 2025. The money is small and the shape is the lesson: a room that happens every week, a job that has to be done during that hour, a price tied to the hour. Clone it for a room you can sit in — a clinic, a classroom, a town hall — not for churches, unless that is your room.
