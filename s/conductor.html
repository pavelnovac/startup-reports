Should you build this

QuickBooks Desktop is not getting a lovely modern API, and the businesses stuck on it still need developers. Conductor charges for that door: $41.5k MRR on TrustMRR, current as of this report. The thing to copy is not QuickBooks. It is any ugly system a trade already refuses to leave — an industry ERP, a desktop POS, an old insurer portal — if you can sit in front of it and speak HTTP.
