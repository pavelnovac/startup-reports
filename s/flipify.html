Should you build this

Resellers already live in Facebook Marketplace, eBay, Craigslist, and OfferUp, looking for a price that is wrong. Flipify scans those listings for them. TrustMRR puts it near $18k MRR, close to the '$21k a month' in a hiring post. Clone it only for a category of goods you know how to resell. A general 'deal finder' is a weekend project with no edge.
